Privacy Policy

Dhikr Counter: Digital Tasbih — com.zikirhane.tesbih

Last updated: 7 September 2026

In short: the app does not ask for an account and does not send your dhikr data anywhere. Everything you count stays on your device. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the app free.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you for a name, e-mail address, phone number, password or any other account information. There is no sign-up and no login. We do not operate a server that stores your dhikr records.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone, using Android's standard app storage, and is readable only by this app:

  • Counter values, laps and completed goals
  • Dhikrs you create, and your favourites
  • Daily goal, streaks and statistics history
  • Preferences: language, theme, sound and vibration, large text, reminder time

Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app, so this data is not copied to Google Drive on its own. If you uninstall the app, the data is removed with it.

3. Backup files you create yourself

The app can export your data to a backup file. This happens only when you ask for it. The file is handed to whatever app you choose to save or send it with, and from that point it is under your control — we neither receive nor store a copy.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process technical data such as:

  • the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it
  • device and app information, and interactions with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as described in its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent screen provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings.

6. Permissions the app requests

  • Internet and network state — only to load ads. All counting features work offline.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback while counting.
  • Keep screen awake — optional, so the screen does not turn off while you count.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no message is sent from a server.

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, precise location or photo library.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your counters and dhikrs from within the app at any time.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID from Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

10. Contact
