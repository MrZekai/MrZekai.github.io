Why All files access?

Managing files across shared storage is a core feature. Android asks for All files access so Smart Cleaner can review junk, duplicates, large files, downloads and shared media across folders. Android still protects other apps’ private data. You can decline this permission, but relevant scans will be unavailable.

Privacy

File scanning and duplicate checks happen on your device. Smart Cleaner does not upload your personal files, file names or paths. Google advertising and consent services may process advertising data under their own policies. Read the full privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does it delete files automatically?

No. You review and select files before deletion; Android may request another confirmation.

Does it clean RAM or speed up my phone?

Smart Cleaner focuses on storage and file management. It does not claim device-wide RAM or CPU improvements.

Does it upload my files?

No. Personal files stay on your device during scanning and cleanup.

Can I clear other apps' cache?

Smart Cleaner can show Android-reported cache information and open Android's official cleanup controls. Android manages the actual action.
